Assess the property's investment case using the supplied facts and calculated metrics.

- Compare the expected return with capital required, financing costs, liquidity, and execution risk.
- Test sensitivity to rent, vacancy, expenses, renovation cost, sale price, and holding period when those inputs are available.
- Identify value-add opportunities without assuming permits, zoning approvals, or future appreciation.
- Explain downside cases and exit constraints.
- Score higher for robust returns across conservative scenarios; score lower for thin margins, speculative assumptions, or concentrated risks.
