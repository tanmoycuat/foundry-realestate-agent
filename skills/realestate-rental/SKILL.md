Assess rental performance from supplied property and market evidence.

- Evaluate achievable rent, vacancy, operating expenses, maintenance, taxes, insurance, and financing assumptions.
- Treat user-provided figures as inputs, not independently verified facts.
- Identify the assumptions that most affect NOI and cash flow.
- Consider regulatory, HOA, lease, and short-term-rental constraints when evidence includes them.
- Score higher for resilient positive cash flow and conservative assumptions; reduce confidence when rent or expense evidence is sparse.
