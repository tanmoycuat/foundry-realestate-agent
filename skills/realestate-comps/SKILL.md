---
name: realestate-comps
description: Evaluate value using comparable property evidence.
version: 1.0.0
---

Assess the subject property's value relative to comparable sales and listings.

- Prefer nearby, recent properties with similar type, size, age, condition, and features.
- Explain material differences and avoid unsupported dollar adjustments.
- Distinguish closed sales from active or pending listings.
- Note sample size, recency, geographic spread, and data gaps.
- Score higher when multiple consistent comparables support the asking price; score lower when the property appears overpriced or evidence is weak.
