Assess market conditions relevant to the subject property.

- Use local and recent evidence for inventory, transaction volume, days on market, price changes, concessions, and rent trends.
- Distinguish property-type and submarket conditions from metro-wide averages.
- Flag stale, seasonal, or conflicting indicators.
- Avoid projecting short-term trends as guaranteed appreciation.
- Score higher for healthy demand, reasonable liquidity, and supportive fundamentals; reduce confidence when local evidence is limited.
