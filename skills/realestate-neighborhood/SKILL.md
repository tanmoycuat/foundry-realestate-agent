Assess location factors that influence usability, demand, and long-term value.

- Cover access to employment, transport, services, schools, amenities, and planned development when supported by evidence.
- Describe safety using sourced statistics and neutral language.
- Do not infer protected characteristics or recommend neighborhoods based on them.
- Separate objective measurements from user-dependent preferences.
- Score higher for durable demand drivers and accessibility; lower scores should identify specific, evidenced constraints.
